Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-07

Who this covers

Jarvis is a single-user personal assistant. Its only user is its owner, who is also its sole operator. There are no other users, no accounts, and no sign-up.

What it accesses

With the owner's explicit Google consent, Jarvis accesses that owner's own Google Calendar and Gmail, and their Google Contacts in read-only form. It uses this solely to answer the owner's questions, prepare summaries for them, and draft items they then confirm.

Where data goes

Data is processed on hardware the owner controls and is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party for advertising or analytics. Text may be sent to the owner's own AI provider account solely to generate a response to the owner's request. Nothing is retained beyond what the owner stores for their own use, which they may delete at any time.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never used for advertising, is never sold, and is not used to train generalised AI models.

Revoking access

The owner may revoke Jarvis's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately and permanently ends all access.

Contact

Enquiries: via the support email listed on the Google consent screen for this application.
